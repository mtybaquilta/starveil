A. Horizontal Space Panorama

Your planet sits at the center, locations spread along a horizontal belt. Scrollable left/right to see more of the galaxy. Each location is a styled tile with CSS illustrations. Detected signals show as pulsing "?" circles. Clicking a location opens the detail panel below.

Key Ideas

  • Home planet is always visible at center with an orbital ring glow
  • Asteroid fields show CSS rock shapes, bandit camps show a station, debris shows metal fragments
  • Detected signals are dashed circles with pulsing "?" — not yet revealed
  • Scrollable horizontally as you discover more coordinates
  • Clicking a location opens a detail panel below (same actions: send probe, view info)
  • Cleared locations fade to low opacity with a "CLEARED" badge
