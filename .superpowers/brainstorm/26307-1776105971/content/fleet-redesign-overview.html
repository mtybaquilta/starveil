B: Power Banner + List Rows

Same power summary at top but ships are shown as horizontal rows instead of cards. Each row shows the ship icon, name, inline stats, available/total count, deployment status, and action buttons. More information-dense — you can see everything at a glance without scrolling. Visually distinct from the Shipyard's card grid.
